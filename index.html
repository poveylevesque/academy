Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the academy.

Who can participate in the academy?

Students of all skill levels are welcome to apply, although previous experience with oil painting is beneficial. International applicants are strongly encouraged to apply. Participants must be at least 18 years old.

How many participants will be in each run of the academy?

Each academy session is limited to a maximum of 26 participants.

What language will the classes be conducted in?

All classes are conducted in English.

How do I register for the academy?

To apply, please email academy@plpa.space with a short, informal introduction about yourself and your artistic practice, along with five images of your work.

What is included in the registration fee?

The registration fee includes an intensive seven-day program, one-on-one mentoring sessions, evening drawing sessions with live models, wine and cheese gatherings, and a final dinner at Edward Povey’s home and studio.

What materials and tools should I bring?

You will receive a list of required materials and supplies before the academy begins.

Will I receive one-on-one mentoring from the instructor?

Yes. Every participant receives individual mentoring sessions with Edward Povey and Suzanne Levesque.

Will the academy help me develop my artistic style?

The academy offers an intensive environment for artistic exploration, practical study, and individual mentoring. Participants have the opportunity to reflect on and develop their artistic practice throughout the program.

Will there be a follow-up community, online group, or future workshops?

Please contact the academy team for the latest information about any follow-up community, online groups, or future workshops.

Is accommodation or travel included in the academy fee?

No. Accommodation and travel expenses are not included in the academy fee. Participants are responsible for arranging and covering their own travel and accommodation.

Can you recommend nearby hotels or accommodation?

Please contact the academy team for recommendations on nearby hotels and accommodation options.
